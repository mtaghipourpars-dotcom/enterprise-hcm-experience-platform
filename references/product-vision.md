# Product Vision

Create a digital human-capital experience layer above real HCM enterprise data.

## Personas

- Employee Experience
- Manager Experience
- Executive Workforce Intelligence

## Core narrative

Organization -> Employee -> Time -> Payroll -> Capability -> Decision

## Domains

1. Personnel Administration (PA)
2. Organizational Management (OM)
3. Time Management (PT)
4. Payroll (PY)
5. Training & Event Management / Learning
6. Talent & Performance Management

The platform is not an SAP GUI replacement and not a static dashboard. It turns enterprise HCM data into role-based services.
