# Talent & Performance Management

## Scope

The platform must support the previously defined Talent & Performance catalog.

## Employee services

- My Goals
- Individual Development Plan (IDP)
- Career Planning
- Succession-related personal profile where authorized
- Self Assessment
- My Appraisal
- Competency and Skill Profile

## Manager services

- Team Goals
- Team Performance
- Feedback/Review
- Development Plans
- Capability/Competency Gaps
- Succession view for authorized roles

## Executive services

- Performance distribution/coverage
- critical capability gaps
- succession coverage
- development pipeline
- talent risk indicators

## SAP alignment

Talent and performance implementations can vary by SAP product/release and may not map 1:1 to classic PA/OM infotype tables. The system must treat the actual target-system source schema as authoritative. Use an object/source mapping registry and `verification_required=true` until DDIC or API metadata confirms the exact source. Do not invent SAP table names for appraisal/goal/talent data.
