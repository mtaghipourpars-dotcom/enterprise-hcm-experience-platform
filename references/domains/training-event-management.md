# Training & Event Management / Personnel Development

## Scope

The platform must support the full catalog previously defined for Training & Event Management.

## Employee services

### Training Center
- current course/event offers
- search/filter
- course/event details
- request or registration
- manager approval when required
- multi-course shopping basket

### My Bookings
- current bookings
- rebooking
- cancellation

### My Training History
- all attended training
- date
- completion status
- result/details

### My Qualifications
- earned qualifications
- required qualifications for current job/position
- gap against required/aspired qualification

### My Training Calendar
- training appointments
- integration-ready calendar events

### Shopping Basket
- add/remove/save/submit multiple learning items

## Manager services

- team training demand
- pending training approvals
- team qualification coverage
- skill/qualification gaps

## Executive services

- workforce qualification coverage
- critical capability gaps
- training demand and completion
- strategic learning indicators

## SAP alignment

Training/Event Management may use SAP Personnel Development / Training & Event Management objects and relationships rather than a single table. The mapping layer must therefore use an object-extract registry and preserve SAP object identity (`OTYPE`, `OBJID`, relationships, validity) where applicable. The exact table/field inventory is release-dependent and must be verified from DDIC before finalizing mappings.
